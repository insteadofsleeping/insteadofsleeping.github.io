Title: Sisyphus
Date: 2026-06-18
Category: Controversy

 One must imagine sisyphus happy— really not  a profound perspective at all. 
Of course he likes pushing the boulder of course he does everyone does and everyone pushes the boulder
You roll it up the hill and you make it nearly to the top and then it rolls down then you can roll it up to the top again and again
Constant reward-deprivation cycle, purpose. Suffering, work, accomplishment, pride. 
What would Sisyphus even do if he did get the boulder to the top? 
I imagine he sits for a while and enjoys his success 
It is all hes ever wanted and he’s Escaped his eternal torment
But he is human and of human nature and soon he wont feel good at all
Soon he will realize he’d rather tip the boulder over and watch it roll all the way down and Start all over again. 

I dont want to push the boulder.
Why is this such a foreign concept to people? 
Sisyphus loved life so much he tried to cheat death. 
Isnt his punishment just a metaphor for life? If you love life so much then live forever— more a lesson than anything. 
If eternal torment is a constant reward-deprivation cycle, purpose; Suffering, work, accomplishment, pride;
Why am i wrong?
I like the idea and i see the vision and 
sometimes it doesnt feel so heavy but 
something in me is programmed wrong and something in me doesnt want to push the boulder and something in me never did. 

I dont want to push the boulder but if i stop everyone suffers and 
Maybe i love them more than i hate life
