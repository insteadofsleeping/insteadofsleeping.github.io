Title: Running
Date: 2026-11-13
Category: Train of thought

I've found that running has become significantly easier twice in my life. First was alongside my pain tolerance skyrocketing when I started antidepressants. Who knew my constant discomfort and pain had a source? I thought everyone experienced life that way, and I was just mentally weak, low endurance, low motivation. When every step stopped feeling like dying, I realized it wasn't about a lack of discipline, effort, and training. 
The second time was when the feeling of strenuous exercise surpassed my resting state in terms of comfort. It's interesting how everything is relative, everything is about perspective. I used to be tired and uncomfortable and running put me in a state where I was more tired and uncomfortable. More recently that fatigue had transformed into a wired, high-energy ball of distress that started in my chest and spread through my body, pulling me into the ground, the opposite of numbness, a sharp pain in my head, where the only thing I could hear was my heart beating, and the only thought I could land on was that I wish that it wasn't. 
I never knew it could get worse than my cold, low-energy, hopeless melancholy pit that I had been dragging myself through my entire life. I figured I had one redeeming quality and that was a lack of anxiety, due to a lack of care for the outcome of anything at all. I always thought it was paradoxical to have both anxiety and depression.
