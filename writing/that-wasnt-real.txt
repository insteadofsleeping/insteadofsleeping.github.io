Title: That wasn’t real
Date: 2024-05-04
Category: Train of thought

I had a dream about a boy whos as good as dead
He wont answer his friends he wont answer his family
Id call them and ask if theres any update
But hes not mine to worry about anymore

I complained about it then, why cant i make new friends? Why should I acknowledge him while hes sulking in the corner? If he wabts to talk to me he can muster up the courage to talk to me
If he thinks im so funny he can find it in him to laugh
To not shoot me down because hes afraid im too good for him

In my dream the knot in his chest seemed to be gone
He laughed at my jokes 
He whooped as i spun the wheel
We were back to our old back and forth and making fun of the houses the cars the people in our lives and the world and everything was a joke again as we hurtled down page mill rd

In my dream i was content
I didnt wish he were any other way
I didnt hold back from him in fear that he would crumble my future
We recounted those nights we got so close but never really really touched and he was too anxious and i wasnt ready so we just sat in each others presence instead
