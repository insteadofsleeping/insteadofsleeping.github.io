Title: Baseless tweak out
Date: 2025-07-28
Category: Train of thought

I keep having these vivid real dreams where either everything is wrong or everyone is angry at me or combination of both. When everything is wrong I am expected to resolve it. Random people I encounter in the street are hostile and hold a severe hatred towards me. My friends start arguments and some yell at me. The faucet is running and won’t turn off and I don’t know what to do and when I ask everyone yells at me for the faucet being on.  Some characters I just know and I don’t know why but I start running and they follow and they chase me down until I wake up. I manifest that I lose them, it’s my dream, why shouldn’t I be able to decide? But most of the time I fail and I have to force myself to wake up before they reach me. 

I have a recurring (day?)dream where I try to surround myself with a hundred people I love and I want to do things for them and drop half a band on fun things and set up the environment to feel on top of the world with me and if I can make people happy I can form this big warm blanket of people I love and maybe it will feel as though they love me back. and if I can’t have love I want admiration or appreciation or anything that makes me feel like anything I do has an impact. 

In this dream my birthday is coming up and everyone loves the idea and sends Yay!! in the group chat and Omg so excited and in this dream the night before my birthday each and every one of my friends individually texts me Im so sorry my mom is super mad at me and won’t let me come and Ugh I can’t make it anymore I have to go to my cousins whatever the fuck and one by one they explain to me that they can’t make it because I am asking too much because it is so difficult and impossible to go eat a free dinner or go on a trip I will literally sponsor despite having no money but I cannot ask too much

And sometimes I wake up expecting relief only to realize this is really happening and I’m turning fifteen and I never thought I would and every second of being fourteen was a fight to exist but im turning fifteen and nobody cares and nobody can make it

And sometimes I wake up and I’m going to turn twenty and I’m begging everyone to come celebrate they don’t really even know what they’re celebrating but if I make it fun maybe they can be coerced into being happy with me or more like stunned and surprised that I am here and that there must have been some benefit in my cost benefit analysis  of life for me to make it past 12 13 14 15 16 17 18 19? And that’s what I’m celebrating is that unknown factor that keeps my hands gripped tight to the rails of the drawbridge and the wheel curving as the road does 

__ begs her mom to let her come camping and her mom asks me __ will you have a birthday next year? I don’t really know what to say besides probably, I never really know

There were days I didn’t think id have a thirteenth and so many days I didn’t think I’d have a fourteenth and so so so soooo many days I didn’t think I’d make fifteen but I did and I always do and they never really care. I don’t expect them to they don’t owe me anything so I bribe them with fun and food and drugs and alcohol and boys and girls and things they love just to lure them into my big warm blanket

Sometimes I wake up expecting relief only to realize this is really happening and everyone is angry at me and won’t tell me why and I am expected to resolve it and I don’t really know how. I feel like I blacked out in the little hole in my head and they peep in to tell me __ we decided you need to vote. Or __ they’re mad and said we ditched them last night.

I have no idea what’s going on and I am immediately defensive because what I just woke up why is everyone mad at me? But I kind of have the sneaking suspicion I might know exactly what’s wrong. I beg for answers but I  am forced to figure it out myself and I confirm that I have committed a moral crime in my haste to get what I want 

I don’t think of myself as a burden more than anyone else I don’t think they would be better off I don’t feel any obligation towards anyone in my self hatred I don’t feel I am saving anyone from my presence and it’s funny that people act like thats a reason when they know it’s not
