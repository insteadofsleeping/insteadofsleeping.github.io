Title: My hottest take
Date: 2025-07-09
Category: Controversy

I dont miss when everything was unbearable
I still cant pretend it wouldnt be easier but at the moment its easier to just keep existing than to find a physically sound way to guarantee i never wake up again 
Its harder than he thinks because he hasnt really thought about it and he doesnt realize i can conceptually believe something and still not have the willpower or ability to fight instincts to do it
i wish it was a click of a button instead of a leap into an abyss or a few years long process or a gorey extended exhibition where my perspectiveless id is given too many chances to escape pain
Its not a sign that i dont want to its like how many people want to be in shape long term but dont want to presently choose healthy food or go to the gym
In the last two weeks did you not at all a few days more than half the days or nearly every day had thoughts of being better off dead? Its a mood questionaire but i dont think that question in particular is a mood
I feel good right now and im happy with myself and my friends and my family and once i finish writing im going back to working on a project i enjoy and then ill go on a hike for fun and then do some art and other hobbies and fun and things i like and enjoy but none of that changes the fact that i know long term i would be better off dead
Thats not something other people can argue against because of course from their perspective theyd be better off with me alive but thats not the question is it?
And they can keep telling me about how i have so much to live for and so many experiences ahead of me and this and that and i listen because I don’t disagree
I know itll be better and i know ill have fun and i know therell be so many moments where i sit and watch the sky change colors and think about how i am glad to be there but regardless if there was an easy way to never experience discomfort anxiety and devastation ever again i would take it. I used to think of it as a scale whether the pros outweigh the cons but noa would always argue that i dont know for a fact that the pros wouldnt outweigh the cons and it would be an unwise gamble to assume that.
I agree but that never changed my mind and i think its because you dont truly weigh things at face value like that.
So maybe its abnormal and labelled a disorder but maybe its like just being tired and not wanting to go to the party and maybe i know all the good i will miss out on and i just dont really care. 
She would say well you might care in the future but if future me doesnt exist then me and her have no qualms
They will tell me it gets better but im doing great and ive been better and ive been happy and i love my life but i just dont want to do itHow can you argue with that?
