Title: committing life
Date: 2026-11-13
Category: Controversy

I used to argue that it was unfair to younger me to forget about the things I suffered. I could forgive or forget but it would never not have happened. My sister fosters her happy healthy relationship with our mother and ignores the large gap in her memory of her childhood. My brain doesn’t let me, but I’m doing my best. 

I guess the real decision comes from committing to life. if I am going to commit to living I must reprogram my brain. I must write my life like a journalist pushing a bubbly narrative. At first it feels wrong, it feels dishonest, it feels unjust to just forget about it forget about it forget about it, to handpick and revise and select how I remember things, how I see everything, but maybe it’s not. Because I guess it’s not wrong, I guess it’s just the exact opposite of the automatic sob story writer that has narrated my life up until now. 

Maybe it’s not fair and maybe not everything needs to be. It’s not truly fair to gift one child their dream toy and not the other, but that does not necessarily take away from the happiness that one child receives. I’ve been taught by activism that suffering should not be forgotten, downplayed, let off the hook. Maybe my personal commitment to life follows a different principle. Whatever it takes to trick my brain into thinking it’s okay to keep holding out, keep pushing, til everyone I love is satisfied with the time I’ve put in. 

I guess even this is me violating that effort, claiming it’s a trick, though I feel I am just being honest. What you put out is what you feed back to yourself, so I’m better off not thinking about it at all. I won’t read this back to myself. I’ll go make my Pinterest board and think of three positive things at the end of each day, with no but’s. I did that the whole summer but all I can remember is how I can’t even find a word to accurately measure how utterly devastated I felt every single day. 

But I’m past that now, and I almost feel like I almost feel okay, and I have felt this way before, and maybe it’ll get bad again but maybe it’ll get better again and maybe one day I’ll even think this decision was worth it. But only I can make that happen. My biggest mistake growing up was thinking someone was going to come along and save me. I kept waiting and waiting and begging the world for something it doesn’t owe me. It took me so long to realize the harsh but simple reality, it’s an ultimatum, you either put in the work, or you don’t.
