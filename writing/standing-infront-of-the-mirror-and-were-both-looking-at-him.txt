Title: Standing infront of the mirror and we’re both looking at him
Date: 2022-10-05
Category: Fiction

i wish he could see me 
the way i see myself
its so devastating
what the twisted mind of the patriarchy 
kept us from becoming
it would have been beautiful

all i see as i get to know him 
are our similarities
he gazes at himself in the mirror
examines every detail
every hair
every curve of the face
every shadow 
of every muscle
and loves every part
he self reflects
and confirms
he is unique
he is competent
he is successful
and he loves himself as he examines
every part of his personality
he watches himself in videos
he replays his every creation
he savors the rewards of his wit and success

and as i examine myself
every hair
every curve of my face
every shadow of every muscle fiber
and as i self reflect
on how smart
how likeable
how talented
how perfect i am
he appears to agree
"you're perfect"
he says
as he compliments
my hair
my face
my body
my face
my hair
and i smile and say "thank you" 
but inside something shatters
because that is hardly me
because there is so much more
why doesnt he see it?
he sees it in himself 
every witty joke
every smart move 
every success
and i see it in myself
and i see it in him
why cant he see it in me?
i am his reflection
but as a woman
he cant look past
the face
and the hair
and the body
to see the perfection of a reflection
that he is so in love with
and i always wonder
if he was able to see it
if he could see me 
the way i see him
the way i see myself
would he have stayed?
maybe the women arent always just a face
maybe the men are the ones 
who cant see past it
