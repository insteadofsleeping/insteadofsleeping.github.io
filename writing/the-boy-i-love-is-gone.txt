Title: The boy i love is gone
Date: 2023-02-15
Category: Fiction

ive seen him do it before, selecting a role, settling into it, playing the role, but its all just a game. the clever creative life of the conversation grows up to be the flirty playboy who everyone wants but no one can have, who dwindles on each girl for a moment providing her support and unknowingly growing her dependence. then the deeply hidden-layered boy who encountered too much too soon becomes the carefree international student living it up in taiwan. where did the painful sleepless nights go? but we still see it, we know he always holds a part of the bay in him. then proves everyone wrong sustaining a two year relationship, putting her first, everyone laughs as he plays relationship guy for the next few years. we know what hes like, though, and when he comes back to visit we see it's still in him, the mischievous carefully crafted words to instigate in the little time he has visiting, the need for excitement, the little interactions, the support that insists on dependence, that again draw the girls in and make them search for more. because he's always there, the boy i love, no matter what role he's playing.

months pass and he doesnt check in anymore. did i quit? am i okay? how are my parents? he knows better than anyone that i may look happy from a distance but it might be my last day. but not a text, a call, nothing. i find out on his last day of break that hes been here, back in the bay, back in our childhood home, 2 minutes from me. with no call. and thats the last time we talk, he asks me to get lunch, and i'm ecstatic, because for the first time i was terrified that the boy i loved was gone, and now thank god he called and asked and now i'll get to see he's really still there and nothing has changed at all.
he forgot he had a meeting, i have to wait for an hour, hes leaving tomorrow, and through our little afterthought of a lunch my words pass right through him. maybe i wasnt there. i ask how hes doing, he answers, and leaves a blank, blaring, empty silence when he finishes and starts looking at his phone, because he doesnt care how im doing, he doesnt ask, he closes off. and its clear, my problems are now my problems, and he wants nothing to do with them. 
and something inside me shatters and spills because i was so excited to tell him how far ife come, i was so excited to tell him im the happiest ive ever been, i was so excited to see him so excited and proud because he was always my number one supporter, my only one supporter, the only one who knew and cared, the only boy i loved. 
and his leg wont stop shaking and he whispers "sorry, what?" twelve times as he cant check his phone and listen at the same time, he cant listen snd think at the same time, he doesnt crack a smile a single time our entire afterthought of a lunch. the dimples that used to never fade, the smile that always connoted mischief, hidden in the folds of the role he's playing.

and even as i write my 16 essay, even as i claim ive moved on and i dont need my role models, i hold on to the hope, the fact that the boy i love is still there, he's just playing a role. i grasp at the hairs he tosses me, he agrees to come to dinner with our fam, he doesnt say a word to me the entire time.
what went so wrong? 
the boy i love couldnt care less how im doing, what im up to, and that im the happiest ive ever been.

and once again im ecstatic when he tosses me a little straw, asks to get lunch which never happens but the intent was there, comes to dinner with my sister and me, trys to listen to maybe one of the things i say. mostly hes dazed, brings up how hes spent $500 on cocaine in the last month and maybe should consider stopping. but again, he doesnt care about my problems, my problems are my problems and his problems are our problems. maybe the boy i love is under stress and is overwhelmed by the role he has to play.

but it was there on the couch of his living room that i finally knew i had to give up. because as he talked about his pasta, his good bowls of pasta, how he can never help but compare them, how he needs them to beg for him, how he could never really care for them, how he hates that they like him after, how he could never fuck a virgin, how he is afraid of getting in a relationship and finding out shes not as good as his bowl of pasta, and no he could never train her, thats not his job, and i sit there and listen and i truly realize, the boy i love is gone. and he can go however far to be fixed, he can be years past college, he can be married and have children but nothing will ever be able to repair this, him and his bowls of pasta are permanently fucked, he will never be able to do anything without comparing his bowls of pasta, his girls he supports and grow dependence are just bowls of pasta, i am just a bowl of pasta. 

the only boy who cared is gone, the only boy who listened is gone, the only boy who understood is gone, the only boy who didn't judge is gone, the only boy i ever loved is gone
