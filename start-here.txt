This is a sample piece, and it doubles as the instructions. Every piece on this site is a plain text file in the folder called **writing**.

# To add a new piece

Make a new text file in the writing folder, for example *my-new-poem.txt*. Use lowercase letters, numbers and dashes in the name, with no spaces.

Start the file with these lines, then leave one blank line, then write:

Title: The name of the piece
Date: 2026-11-10
Category: Poems

# Categories

Whatever you type after *Category:* becomes a section of the site, listed across the top of the home page with a count. Spell a category the same way each time and its pieces are filed together. The three in these samples (Journal, Notes, Poems) are only examples; yours appear as soon as you use them.

You can also add a line such as *Label: Sonnet* for a finer description. It is optional.

Then open *list.txt* in the same folder and add the file's name on its own line. Upload both files to your host and the piece appears, with its read time worked out for you.

# Small touches

Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Line breaks inside a paragraph are kept, which is what poems need. Put one star on each side of a phrase for *italics* and two for **bold**. A line that starts with a # and a space becomes a small heading.

The newest piece by date always sits in the purple block at the top of the home page.
